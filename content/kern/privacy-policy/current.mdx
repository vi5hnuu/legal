# Privacy Policy for Kern

_Last updated: November 26, 2026 · Effective: November 26, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how Kern ("We", "Us", "Our"), made by Laxmi Solutions, handles your information when you use the Kern app. In short: **your documents stay on your phone**. Kern has no account system, no servers of its own that receive your documents, and no analytics. Crash reports, which you can turn off, help us fix bugs (section 2.12). The optional AI assistant in Kern Pro sends the text you ask it about to the AI provider you connect, and only when you ask (section 2.13). The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (section 2.11); **Kern Pro has no ads**.

---

## 1. What is Kern?

Kern is a Typst editor for Android. You write documents, and Kern compiles them to PDF **on your device** with the built-in Typst compiler. Optional features let you keep projects in a folder you choose, sync them with Git or Google Drive, and, with Kern Pro, turn a photo or drawing of a formula into Typst (Snap math), import LaTeX projects as Typst, and use an optional AI assistant that you connect with your own API key.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

**We do not collect personal information**, apart from the crash reports described in section 2.12. Kern does not ask you to sign up, and it contains no analytics SDK. It uses Google's Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports, which you can turn off, and the free version includes Google's advertising SDK, which is described in section 2.11. The sections below explain what stays on your device and the network connections Kern makes on your behalf.

### 2.1 Your Projects and Settings (On Your Device)

Your projects (`.typ` files, bibliographies, images, fonts and other files), compiled PDFs and settings are stored **in Kern's private storage on your phone**. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them, unless you keep them in a projects folder (section 2.2), they are kept in an Android backup (section 2.9), or you sync them (sections 2.5 and 2.6).

### 2.2 Projects Folder and Opening Files (Optional)

If you choose a **projects folder**, Kern saves a copy of your projects there as ordinary files and picks up changes made to them by other apps. Kern can access **only the folder you pick** in Android's folder picker, and you can stop using it at any time in **Settings → Projects folder**; the files already in the folder stay there. When you open a `.typ` file or a LaTeX project from another app or the file picker, Kern copies it into a new project. Kern opens nothing on your device that you did not choose.

### 2.3 Compiling and Typst Packages

Documents are compiled **entirely on your device**. When a document imports a package from Typst Universe (for example `#import "@preview/cetz:…"`), Kern downloads that package from the Typst package server (`packages.typst.org`, run by the Typst project) and keeps it on your device so later compiles work offline. To suggest packages on the Insert screen, Kern also downloads the public package list from the same server about once a day. These requests contain only the names and versions of the packages; **your document's content is never sent**. Like any web server, the package server can see your IP address and the time of the request.

You can clear downloaded packages in **Settings → Downloads**.

### 2.4 Downloads Through Google Play

To keep the app small, the Snap math feature and the software the AI assistant needs to reach AI providers are delivered by **Google Play** as optional downloads, and the app may check Google Play for updates. These downloads are handled by Google Play under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 2.5 Git (Optional)

If you clone a repository or connect a project to Git, Kern talks **directly to the Git host you choose** (for example GitHub or GitLab) over HTTPS. If you enter a username and access token, they are stored **only on your device**, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore, and are sent only to that Git host. Commits you push include the author name you set and contain your project files, as with any Git client. You can remove saved credentials in **Settings → Git**.

### 2.6 Google Drive (Optional)

If you turn on Google Drive sync for a project, Kern asks for the **`drive.file`** permission, which lets the app see and manage **only the files and folders it creates** in your Drive, not your other Drive files. Project files are copied **directly between your device and Google Drive**; they never pass through our servers. Kern stores the email address of the connected Google account on your device so it can show which account is connected.

**Kern's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.** We do not use Google Drive data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not use it to train AI models.

You can turn sync off per project in **Share & sync**, and revoke access at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

### 2.7 Snap Math, LaTeX Import and the Document Scanner (Kern Pro)

Formula recognition runs **on your device** with a bundled machine-learning model, and the result is converted to Typst on your device; images are not uploaded. LaTeX projects you import are also converted to Typst on your device. The camera scanner is **Google's ML Kit document scanner**, provided by Google Play services, which processes the scan on your device and hands Kern only the resulting image. Kern does not request the camera permission itself.

### 2.8 Purchases

Kern Pro is a one-time purchase through **Google Play Billing**. Google handles payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether Kern Pro is owned by your Google account and remembers the answer on your device so Pro features work offline.

### 2.9 Android Backup

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up your projects and settings to your Google account, under Google's terms. Downloaded Typst packages, the Snap model, your Git and Drive credentials, and your AI provider API keys and AI settings are excluded from backups.

### 2.10 Ratings

After you have used Kern for a while, the app may ask Google Play to show its in-app rating prompt. Your rating and review go to Google Play; we do not receive them through the app.

### 2.11 Advertising (Free Version Only)

The free version of Kern shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**: a banner on the project and template lists, and occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks (for example after closing a document or when you return to the app), never while you are writing. **Kern Pro shows no ads, and the advertising SDK does not run for Pro users.**

To show, measure and limit ads, Google AdMob collects information from your device, such as your **advertising ID**, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This information goes **directly to Google**; we do not receive it, and your documents are never shared with advertisers. Google's use of this data is described in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Ads are chosen using general topics related to Kern (such as mathematics and academic writing). Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Kern asks for your **consent** before any ad is requested, using Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Ad privacy choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's **Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**). Kern limits ads to content suitable for general audiences.

### 2.12 Crash Reports

To find and fix bugs, Kern uses **Firebase Crashlytics**, a service provided by Google. When the app crashes, or when something important fails (for example, Google Play can't install the Snap math feature), a report is sent to Crashlytics. It contains the technical details of the error (such as the stack trace and error codes), your device model, Android version, app version, available memory and storage, whether the app was open on screen, and a random installation identifier created by Firebase. Reports **never include your documents, file or project names, or account details**, and they are not used for advertising.

Google processes crash reports on our behalf under the [Firebase privacy and security terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), and they are deleted after 90 days. You can turn crash reports off at any time in **Settings → About → Send crash reports**; nothing more is sent until you turn them back on.

### 2.13 AI Assistant (Kern Pro, Optional)

Kern Pro includes an optional AI assistant that you can ask to write, change, fix or explain things in the file you are editing, including the errors from a failed compile. It is **off until you set it up**: you choose an AI provider (Anthropic's Claude, OpenAI's ChatGPT, Google's Gemini, or a compatible server whose address you enter) and enter **your own API key** for it. We do not provide AI accounts or keys, and we operate no server between you and the provider.

**What is sent, and when.** Nothing is sent until you ask the assistant for something. Each request goes **directly from your device to the provider you chose**, over HTTPS, and contains:

- your API key, so the provider can identify your account;
- what you asked, and the earlier questions and answers of the same conversation;
- the file you have open in the editor, with its name, the position of your cursor and the text you have selected. A very large file is sent in part: its opening lines, up to the first heading, and the lines around your cursor and around compile errors;
- the error messages of the last compile, with the compiler's hints and the names of the files they are in;
- the keys of citations that have no bibliography entry yet, so that entries can be drafted for them when you ask;
- an image, only when you attach one to your question. You choose it with the Android photo picker; Kern cannot see your other photos, and sends a reduced-size copy;
- the name of the AI model you chose.

The other files of your project are not sent. When you enter or change a key, Kern asks the provider for its list of models, which sends only the key. When you choose a model, Kern sends it a one-word test message to check that it works with your key. Like any web server, the provider can see your IP address and the time of each request.

**What the provider does with it.** The provider processes your request under **its own terms and privacy policy and your account with it**, including how long it keeps requests and whether it uses them to improve its services. We are not a party to that and cannot see, change or delete it. Please read your provider's policy: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy), [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/), [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy). For a custom server, the operator of that server receives the request.

**What we receive.** Nothing. We do not receive your API key, your requests or the answers, and Kern keeps no history of them: an answer is shown on screen and is gone when you close it, unless you insert it into your document.

**Your API key** is stored **only on your device**, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore, is left out of Android backups, and is sent only to the provider it belongs to. You can remove it at any time in **Settings → AI assistant**. Usage is billed by the provider to your account with it.

**Reporting an answer.** Each answer has a **Report this response** option, which opens an email to us in your email app. It contains the provider and model names; anything else, including the answer itself, is included only if you add it.

## 3. Permissions

- **Internet and network state** — to download Typst packages and the package list, Git and Drive sync, Google Play downloads and purchases, crash reports, ads in the free version, and requests you make to your AI provider (section 2.13).
- **Advertising ID** (`AD_ID`) — declared by Google's advertising SDK so ads can be measured and limited in the free version (section 2.11).

Kern opens files and folders only through Android's file and folder pickers, or when you open a file with Kern from another app, so it can read only what you choose.

---

## 4. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. The only services Kern communicates with are the ones you use: Google Play (downloads, updates, purchases, ratings), Google Drive (if you enable it), your Git host (if you use Git), the Typst package server (for packages and the package list), Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports, unless you turn them off; section 2.12) and, in the free version, Google AdMob (section 2.11), which collects the advertising data described there directly from your device. If you set up the AI assistant, the text you ask it about goes to the AI provider you chose, at your request (section 2.13).

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Everything Kern stores is on your device or in places you choose. You can delete a project in the app, clear downloaded packages in Settings, remove Git credentials and AI provider API keys in Settings, or clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove everything in the app. Files in your projects folder, and data you pushed to a Git host or synced to Google Drive, stay there until you delete them. Crash reports are deleted by Firebase Crashlytics after 90 days. Text you sent to an AI provider is kept or deleted by that provider under its own policy.

---

## 6. Security

Network connections use HTTPS. Git tokens and AI provider API keys are encrypted with keys held in the Android Keystore. Projects are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read; a projects folder you choose is readable by apps you allow to access it. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we design Kern to keep your data on your device.

---

## 7. Children

Kern is a general-purpose writing tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. Ads in the free version are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

## 8. Your Rights

Apart from crash reports (section 2.12), which are not linked to your name, email or accounts and are deleted after 90 days, we do not hold your personal data, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You can stop crash reports at any time in **Settings → About → Send crash reports**. For data held by Google (Play, Drive, backups, AdMob), your Git host or your AI provider, please use those services' controls; you can change your ad consent in **Settings → Ad privacy choices** and manage your advertising ID in your device settings. If you have any question, contact us below.
