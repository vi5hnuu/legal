# Privacy Policy for Lemma

_Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Effective: October 31, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how Lemma ("We", "Us", "Our"), made by Laxmi Solutions, handles your information when you use the Lemma app. In short: **your documents stay on your phone**. Lemma has no account system, no servers of its own that receive your documents, and no analytics. Crash reports, which you can turn off, help us fix bugs (section 2.12). The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (section 2.10); **Lemma Pro has no ads**.

---

## 1. What is Lemma?

Lemma is a LaTeX editor for Android. You write documents, and Lemma compiles them to PDF **on your device** with a built-in TeX engine. Optional features let you keep projects in a folder you choose, sync them with Git or Google Drive, and Lemma Pro adds Snap math (turning a photo or drawing of a formula into LaTeX) and conversion to Typst.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

**We do not collect personal information**, apart from the crash reports described in section 2.12. Lemma does not ask you to sign up, and it contains no analytics SDK. It uses Google's Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports, which you can turn off, and the free version includes Google's advertising SDK, which is described in section 2.10. The sections below explain what stays on your device and the network connections Lemma makes on your behalf.

### 2.1 Your Projects and Settings (On Your Device)

Your projects (`.tex`, `.bib`, images and other files), compiled PDFs and settings are stored **in Lemma's private storage on your phone**. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them, unless you keep them in a projects folder (section 2.11), they are kept in an Android backup (section 2.8), or you sync them (sections 2.4 and 2.5).

### 2.2 Compiling and TeX Packages

Documents are compiled **entirely on your device**. The first time a document uses a TeX package (for example `amsmath`), Lemma downloads that package's files from a TeX package server (the Tectonic project's distribution server, or a mirror operated by us) and keeps them on your device so later compiles work offline. These requests contain only the names of the package files needed; **your document's content is never sent**. Like any web server, the package server can see your IP address and the time of the request.

You can see and clear downloaded packages in **Settings → Downloads**.

### 2.3 Downloads Through Google Play

To keep the app small, the offline compiler and the Snap math feature are delivered by **Google Play** as optional downloads, and the app may check Google Play for updates. These downloads are handled by Google Play under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 2.4 Git (Optional)

If you clone a repository or connect a project to Git, Lemma talks **directly to the Git host you choose** (for example GitHub or GitLab) over HTTPS. If you enter a username and access token, they are stored **only on your device**, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore, and are sent only to that Git host. Commits you push include the author name you set and contain your project files, as with any Git client. You can remove saved credentials in **Settings → Sync → Git**.

### 2.5 Google Drive (Optional)

If you turn on Google Drive sync for a project, Lemma asks for the **`drive.file`** permission, which lets the app see and manage **only the files and folders it creates** in your Drive, not your other Drive files. Project files are copied **directly between your device and Google Drive**; they never pass through our servers. Lemma stores the email address of the connected Google account on your device so it can show which account is connected.

**Lemma's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.** We do not use Google Drive data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not use it to train AI models.

You can turn sync off per project in **Share & sync**, and revoke access at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

### 2.6 Snap Math and the Document Scanner (Lemma Pro)

Formula recognition runs **on your device** with a bundled machine-learning model; images are not uploaded. The camera scanner is **Google's ML Kit document scanner**, provided by Google Play services, which processes the scan on your device and hands Lemma only the resulting image. Lemma does not request the camera permission itself.

### 2.7 Purchases

Lemma Pro is a one-time purchase through **Google Play Billing**. Google handles payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether Lemma Pro is owned by your Google account and remembers the answer on your device so Pro features work offline.

### 2.8 Android Backup

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up your projects and settings to your Google account, under Google's terms. Downloaded TeX packages, the Snap model, and your Git and Drive credentials are excluded from backups.

### 2.9 Ratings

After you have used Lemma for a while, the app may ask Google Play to show its in-app rating prompt. Your rating and review go to Google Play; we do not receive them through the app.

### 2.10 Advertising (Free Version Only)

The free version of Lemma shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**: a banner on the project and template lists, and occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks (for example after closing a document), never while you are writing. **Lemma Pro shows no ads, and the advertising SDK does not run for Pro users.**

To show, measure and limit ads, Google AdMob collects information from your device, such as your **advertising ID**, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This information goes **directly to Google**; we do not receive it, and your documents are never shared with advertisers. Google's use of this data is described in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Ads are chosen using general topics related to Lemma (such as mathematics and academic writing). Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Lemma asks for your **consent** before any ad is requested, using Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Ad privacy choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's **Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**). Lemma limits ads to content suitable for general audiences.

### 2.11 Projects Folder and Opening Files (Optional)

If you choose a **projects folder**, Lemma saves a copy of your projects there as ordinary files and picks up changes made to them by other apps. Lemma can access **only the folder you pick** in Android's folder picker, and you can stop using it at any time in **Settings → Projects folder**; the files already in the folder stay there. When you open a `.tex` file with Lemma from another app, Lemma copies it into a new project.

### 2.12 Crash Reports

To find and fix bugs, Lemma uses **Firebase Crashlytics**, a service provided by Google. When the app crashes, or when something important fails (for example, Google Play can't install the offline compiler), a report is sent to Crashlytics. It contains the technical details of the error (such as the stack trace and error codes), your device model, Android version, app version, available memory and storage, whether the app was open on screen, and a random installation identifier created by Firebase. Reports **never include your documents, file or project names, or account details**, and they are not used for advertising.

Google processes crash reports on our behalf under the [Firebase privacy and security terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), and they are deleted after 90 days. You can turn crash reports off at any time in **Settings → About → Send crash reports**; nothing more is sent until you turn them back on.

## 3. Permissions

- **Internet and network state** — to download TeX packages, Git and Drive sync, Google Play downloads and purchases, crash reports, ads in the free version, and to download the offline compiler only on Wi-Fi.
- **Advertising ID** (`AD_ID`) — declared by Google's advertising SDK so ads can be measured and limited in the free version (section 2.10).

Lemma opens files and folders only through Android's file and folder pickers, or when you open a file with Lemma from another app, so it can read only what you choose.

---

## 4. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. The only services Lemma communicates with are the ones you use: Google Play (downloads, updates, purchases, ratings), Google Drive (if you enable it), your Git host (if you use Git), the TeX package server (for package files), Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports, unless you turn them off; section 2.12) and, in the free version, Google AdMob (section 2.10), which collects the advertising data described there directly from your device.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Everything Lemma stores is on your device or in places you choose. You can delete a project in the app, clear downloaded packages in Settings, remove Git credentials in Settings, or clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove everything in the app. Files in your projects folder, and data you pushed to a Git host or synced to Google Drive, stay there until you delete them. Crash reports are deleted by Firebase Crashlytics after 90 days.

---

## 6. Security

Network connections use HTTPS. Git tokens are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. Projects are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read; a projects folder you choose is readable by apps you allow to access it. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we design Lemma to keep your data on your device.

---

## 7. Children

Lemma is a general-purpose writing tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. Ads in the free version are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

---

## 8. Your Rights

Apart from crash reports (section 2.12), which are not linked to your name, email or accounts and are deleted after 90 days, we do not hold your personal data, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You can stop crash reports at any time in **Settings → About → Send crash reports**. For data held by Google (Play, Drive, backups, AdMob) or your Git host, please use those services' controls; you can change your ad consent in **Settings → Ad privacy choices** and manage your advertising ID in your device settings. If you have any question, contact us below.
