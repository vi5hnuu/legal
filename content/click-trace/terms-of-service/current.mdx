## 2. Description of Service

Click Trace is a free extension for Google Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers that runs **entirely in your browser**. It lets you:

- Inspect the box model, dimensions, coordinates, computed CSS and accessibility details of elements on a web page.
- Measure with a pixel ruler and guides, and check spacing between elements.
- Pick colours and check colour contrast.
- Show layout grids and element outlines, and compare a page with a design mockup overlay.
- Take screenshots of the visible part of the current tab.

Click Trace only runs on a tab after you open it there. It does not change the pages you inspect; its overlay is removed when you close it.
