## 2. Description of Service

Xylophone is a musical toy app that runs **entirely on your device**. It lets you:

- Tap seven colourful keys to play real instrument notes.
- Switch between three octaves (Low, Mid, High) and adjust the volume.
- Record a short tune and play it back.

Xylophone has **no accounts** and stores tunes and settings only on your device.
