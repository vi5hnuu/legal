## 2. Description of Service

Reflex is an Android automation app that runs **entirely on your device**. It lets you:

- Trigger actions from gestures and device events (shake, flip, pick-up, taps on the floating button, volume keys, charging, connectivity changes, and more).
- Run actions such as taking a screenshot, toggling Do Not Disturb, launching apps, adjusting brightness, and other device controls.
- Chain actions into multi-step workflows with optional conditions.
- Unlock additional capacity and features via a one-time **Reflex Pro** purchase.

Reflex requires the Android **Accessibility Service** to detect gestures and perform actions. It uses this service solely for automation and does not read or transmit your screen content.
