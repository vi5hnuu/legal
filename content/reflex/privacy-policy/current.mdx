# Privacy Policy for Reflex

_Last updated: June 19, 2026 · Effective: June 12, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how Reflex ("We", "Us", "Our") handles your information when you use our application. By using Reflex, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. What is Reflex?

Reflex is an Android automation app that turns gestures and device events into actions — for example, shake to take a screenshot, or flip face-down to enable Do Not Disturb. Reflex runs **entirely on your device**. There is no Reflex account and no Reflex server: your shortcuts, settings, and run history never leave your phone except through Google's services described below.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Data
**None.** Reflex has no sign-up and no login. We do not collect your name, email, or any account identifier.

### 2.2 Shortcuts, Settings & Run History
The shortcuts you build, your app settings, and a capped history of recent shortcut runs (shortcut name, icon, trigger, and timestamp) are stored **only in your device's private app storage**. This data is never transmitted to us.

### 2.3 Accessibility Service Data
Reflex hosts its automation engine inside an Android Accessibility Service so it can detect the gestures you configure and perform the actions you configure (such as taking a screenshot or locking the screen). Reflex **does not read, log, store, or transmit the content of your screen, the apps you use, or anything you type.**

### 2.4 Sensor & Device-State Data
To detect triggers, Reflex reads sensors and system state in real time — accelerometer, proximity, and step sensors; screen, charging, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, ringer, and Do Not Disturb state; and, for the optional call triggers, phone state. These readings are processed **in memory on your device** and are never stored or sent.

### 2.5 Location Data
Location is used **only** by the optional "At a location" condition, to compare your current area against coordinates you enter. Reflex requests **coarse** location, checks it on-device at the moment a shortcut fires, and does **not** store your location, track you in the background, or transmit your location anywhere.

### 2.6 Microphone & Audio Recordings
The optional **Record-audio** action (a Reflex Pro feature) captures microphone audio only when you trigger it. While recording, Reflex runs a visible **microphone foreground service** with a persistent notification so you always know capture is active, and recording stops as soon as you toggle the action off. Reflex requests the **microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)** permission the first time you use this action.

Recordings are saved as `.m4a` files to your device's **shared Music storage** (a "Reflex" collection), so you can find and play them in your own file manager or media apps. These files **stay on your device** — Reflex never uploads, reads, or transmits them, and you can delete them at any time from your file manager. Reflex does not record audio at any other time.

### 2.7 Purchase Data
If you buy **Reflex Pro** (a one-time in-app purchase), the transaction is handled by **Google Play Billing**. Reflex verifies your entitlement on-device through Google Play and never receives or stores your payment details.

### 2.8 Advertising Data
Free users see ads served by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address, and usage signals to serve and measure ads, in accordance with [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). In the European Economic Area and the UK, Reflex shows a Google-certified consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are served. Purchasing Reflex Pro removes all ads.

---

## 4. Data We Do NOT Collect

Reflex is explicitly designed so that:

- **Your screen content never leaves your device.** The Accessibility Service is used only to detect gestures and run your actions — it does not capture or transmit what is on screen.
- **Your sensor and location data stay on your device.** They are evaluated in the moment and discarded.
- **Your audio recordings stay on your device.** Recordings made by the Record-audio action are saved only to your device's storage and are never uploaded to us.
- **We have no account and no server**, so we hold no profile of you.

## 7. Your Rights

Because Reflex stores your data only on your device, you control it directly:
- **Access / delete** your shortcuts and history in-app, or remove everything by clearing app storage or uninstalling.
- **Delete your audio recordings** at any time from your device's file manager or media app (they live in your shared Music storage).
- **Withdraw ad consent** at any time (purchase Reflex Pro to go ad-free), and adjust ad personalisation via your device's Google settings.
- **Revoke any permission** in Android Settings; the related feature simply stops working.

### India — Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP)
We process your data only for the purposes described above and support correction or erasure requests at the email below.

### European / UK Users (GDPR / UK GDPR)
You additionally have rights to object to processing and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. EEA/UK ad consent is collected via Google's User Messaging Platform.

## 8. Data Security

- No screen content, sensor data, or location is transmitted off-device — the strongest possible protection for that data.
- Communication with Google Play and AdMob uses encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.
