# Privacy Policy for Chatcraft

_Last updated: May 30, 2026 · Effective: May 30, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how Chatcraft ("We", "Us", "Our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our application. By using Chatcraft, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. What is Chatcraft?

Chatcraft is a mobile application that lets you convert WhatsApp chat exports into beautifully formatted PDF memory books. All chat processing happens **entirely on your device** — your conversation data is never uploaded to our servers.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Data
When you register, we collect:
- Email address
- First and last name (optional)
- Username (chosen by you)
- Password (stored as a secure hash — never in plaintext)

You may also sign in using Google, in which case we receive your Google profile name and email via OAuth.

### 2.2 Chat Export Data
WhatsApp exports (`.txt` files and `.zip` archives with media) that you import into Chatcraft are processed **locally on your device only**. This data is stored in your device's private app storage and is never transmitted to our servers.

### 2.3 Generated PDFs
PDFs you generate are saved to your device. We do not have access to, nor do we store, the content of your generated PDF files.

### 2.4 Purchase Data
If you purchase export credits via Google Play, we store:
- Your Google Play **purchase token** and **Order ID**, used solely to verify your credit balance with the Google Play Developer API.
- The timestamp and quantity of each purchase.
- This information is never shared with third parties beyond Google Play verification.

### 2.5 Usage and Analytics Data
We use Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) to collect anonymised usage data such as:
- Screens visited and features used
- App version and device type
- Session duration and crash reports

This data is aggregated and cannot identify you individually.

### 2.6 Advertising Data
We use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements to free-tier users (users with zero export credits). AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage signals to serve relevant ads, in accordance with [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). No ads are shown to users who have purchased export credits.

### 2.7 Guest Accounts
Guest accounts (no email required) store a temporary user record. **All data associated with a guest account is permanently deleted 7 days after creation.**

## 3. How We Use Your Data

| Purpose | Legal Basis |
|---|---|
| Providing the app and managing your account | Contract performance |
| Verifying export credit purchases with Google Play | Contract performance |
| Improving the app (Firebase Analytics) | Legitimate interest |
| Serving ads to free users (AdMob) | Legitimate interest / consent |
| Complying with legal obligations | Legal obligation |

We do **not** use your data to:
- Read or process your chat exports on our servers
- Build advertising profiles based on your chat content
- Sell your personal data to third parties

---

## 4. Data We Do NOT Collect

Chatcraft is explicitly designed so that:

- **Your WhatsApp chats never leave your device.** The `.txt` or `.zip` file you import is parsed and processed entirely on-device. No chat content is uploaded.
- **Your generated PDFs stay on your device.** We do not have access to the PDF files you create.
- **We cannot read your conversations.** We have no technical capability to access your chat data.

---

## 5. Sharing Your Data

We share data only in the following circumstances:

| Recipient | What is shared | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Google LLC** | Analytics events, AdMob identifiers, Play purchase tokens | Analytics, advertising, purchase verification |
| **Law enforcement / courts** | Account metadata (not chat content) | Legal obligation |
| **Acquirer (if Company is sold)** | Account data | Business transfer — users will be notified |

We never share your chat content because we never have access to it.

## 6. Data Retention

| Data type | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account data | Until account deletion |
| Guest account data | 7 days from creation |
| Purchase tokens | As long as the account exists |
| Firebase Analytics events | Per Google's data retention settings (default 14 months) |
| Chat exports and generated PDFs | Stored locally on your device; deleted when you uninstall the app or clear storage |

You have the right to:
- **Access** your personal data
- **Correct** inaccurate account data
- **Delete** your account and all associated server-side data (via Settings → Delete Account or by contacting us)
- **Withdraw consent** for ads at any time (purchase export credits to go ad-free)

In accordance with India's DPDP Act:
- We process your data only for the purposes described above.
- You have the right to request correction or erasure of your personal data.
- Contact us at the email below to exercise these rights.

### European Users (GDPR)
If you access the Service from within the European Economic Area, you additionally have rights to data portability and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

## 8. Data Security

We implement:
- HTTPS/TLS for all API communication
- BCrypt hashing for passwords
- HTTP-only session cookies
- Your chat data never leaves your device — the strongest possible data security

## 9. Children's Privacy

Chatcraft is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has registered, we will delete their account immediately.
