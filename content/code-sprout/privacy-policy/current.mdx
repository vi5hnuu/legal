# Privacy Policy for Code Sprout

_Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Effective: October 23, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how **Code Sprout** ("We", "Us", "Our"), operated by **Laxmi Solutions**, collects, uses, retains, and protects your personal data when you use the Code Sprout website at [https://code-sprout.laxmi.solutions](https://code-sprout.laxmi.solutions) and the APIs that back it (together, the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

This Policy is written to meet the requirements of India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDPA")**. In this Policy, "You" refers to the **Data Principal** and we refer to ourselves as the **Data Fiduciary**.

---

## 1. What is Code Sprout?

Code Sprout is a coding-practice website: tutorials and articles, a library of practice problems with an in-browser editor and automatic judge, study plans, a comment and bookmark layer, and a Studio for authoring new content. You can read anonymously; you need an account to run and submit code, bookmark problems, comment, or publish content.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you give us when you create an account

Our shared identity service collects:

- **Email address**
- **Username** (optional at signup, you can set/change it later)
- **Password**, stored only as a BCrypt hash — the plaintext is never written anywhere on our side

You may instead sign in with **Google**, in which case we receive, through Google Identity Services, the following from your Google account:

- Your **Google-verified email address**
- Your **name** and **profile picture** URL as recorded on your Google account

We do not request any additional Google scopes beyond standard OpenID Connect profile data.

### 2.2 Information you give us by using Code Sprout

- Content you author in the Studio: articles, problem statements, test sets, solutions, videos, study plans.
- Code you run or submit against practice problems. Your submissions and their verdicts are stored in your account's submission history.
- Comments and replies you post.
- Private notes you save against a problem (visible only to you).
- Bookmarks and the lists you organise them in (private by default).
- Likes, follows, and ratings you give.
- Your display name, handle, bio and avatar as you edit them on your profile.
- Reports you submit about content or users.

### 2.3 Information we observe

Our servers log a limited amount of technical data per request:

- HTTP method, path, response status, latency, and a request id (also returned as `X-Request-ID`)
- Your IP address (used for rate limiting and the anonymous-tier per-IP quota)
- Structured application logs, kept on rolling daily files and rotated out after approximately 30 days

We do **not** log request bodies, your password, your session token, or the contents of your code submissions in these access logs.

## 3. Why We Collect It (Purposes under DPDPA, 2023)

We collect the data above for these specific, limited purposes:

| Purpose | What we use |
|---|---|
| Create and secure your account | email, password hash, Google profile (if OAuth), IP, session cookies |
| Judge the code you submit | submission source, selected language, problem id, verdict and timing |

| Let you contribute content | articles, problems, test sets, videos you author |

We do **not** sell your personal data. We do, however, show third-party advertisements via **Google AdSense** to help cover the cost of running the Service — see Section 6a below for exactly what AdSense receives and how to opt out of personalisation.

## 4. Consent (DPDPA Section 4–5)

We rely on your **consent** as the lawful basis for most of this processing. You give consent by creating an account and ticking the checkbox at sign-up that confirms you have read and agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.

Your consent is:

- **Specific** — covering the purposes listed in Section 3 above
- **Informed** — through this Policy, which we present at the point of sign-up
- **Free and unconditional** — you can decline, in which case you can still browse the site without an account (anonymous access is supported)
- **Unambiguous** — through an affirmative check-box, not pre-ticked
- **Withdrawable** — see Section 7 (Your Rights)

Processing that is **necessary for a legitimate use** under Section 7 of the DPDPA (for example, security logging, fraud prevention, and complying with legal obligations) does not require your consent and is covered by the lawful bases in the Act.

## 5. Children (DPDPA Section 9)

The Service is **not intended for users under 18**. During sign-up we ask you to confirm that you are 18 or older. If we learn that an account belongs to a child, we will suspend it and delete the account's personal data as soon as reasonably possible.

We do not knowingly track children or process their personal data for any secondary purpose. Advertising on the Service runs through Google AdSense in **non-personalised mode** for any reader we have reason to believe may be a child.

If you believe a child has created an account, write to our Grievance Officer (Section 13) and we will act within statutory timelines.

## 6a. Advertising via Google AdSense

Code Sprout displays third-party advertisements via **Google AdSense** (publisher ID `pub-4715945578201106`) to help cover hosting, judge-execution and bandwidth costs for a service we offer free.

### What Google receives

When an ad renders on a page you visit, Google receives: your IP address, user-agent, the URL of the current page, and — if you have Google's advertising cookies — identifiers from your Google advertising profile. Google uses these to pick which ad to show you.

### Cookies set by AdSense

AdSense sets its own cookies and uses the Interest-Based Advertising framework. These cookies are **third-party**, set under `*.google.com` and `*.doubleclick.net`, and are **not** read by us. They are used for ad frequency capping, click measurement, and (unless you opt out) personalisation.

### How to opt out of personalised ads

- **This site, immediately:** append `?ab=0` to any URL and the next page view opts you out for the session, or
- **Everywhere Google serves ads:** [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) → "Personalised ads" → off, or
- **In the EU / UK / Swiss consent flow:** the IAB TCF prompt we show on first visit lets you decline personalisation; your choice persists via a first-party cookie.

Non-personalised ads still show, but they are chosen only from the current page's content, not from your Google ad profile.

### Children

Any session we have reason to believe is from a child (because the signed-in account self-declared under 18, or the device opts in to Chrome's child-safe browsing) is served ads in **non-personalised mode** regardless of the above.

### Full policy

Google's own policy on how AdSense uses the data it collects is at [policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 9. How We Protect Your Data (DPDPA Section 8(5))

- Passwords are hashed with **BCrypt** before storage. The plaintext never leaves memory.
- Sign-in tokens are **RS256-signed JWTs** issued by our identity service; token audience and expiry are checked on every request.
- Session cookies are **HttpOnly**, **Secure**, and `SameSite=Lax`.
- All traffic between you and the Service is over **HTTPS** with HSTS enforced.
- Code submissions are executed inside **isolated Firecracker microVMs** on ls-judge, not on shared machines.
- The public bucket is served only through a CloudFront distribution; the private bucket (test sets, checkers, archived revisions) is reachable only via time-limited presigned URLs we mint for authorised actions.
- Access to production data is limited to the Laxmi Solutions operations team.
- Our APIs are rate-limited per IP and per account; abusive traffic is blocked at the edge.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach of your personal data occurs, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India without unreasonable delay, as required by DPDPA Section 8(6).

## 10. Cookies and Local Storage

We use a small number of **first-party** cookies and `localStorage` entries, all strictly for site function:

- **Session cookie** — a short-lived JWT that keeps you signed in.
- **Theme preference** — remembers whether you chose light or dark mode.
- **Draft and editor state** — your in-progress code and article drafts, stored in `localStorage` so you don't lose work if your tab reloads.

In addition, Google AdSense (Section 6a) sets its own **third-party cookies** under `*.google.com` and `*.doubleclick.net` to cap ad frequency and (unless you opt out) personalise ads. Google Identity Services sets its own cookies only while the sign-in widget is open.
