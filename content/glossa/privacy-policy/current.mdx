# Privacy Policy for Glossa

_Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Effective: November 4, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how Glossa ("We", "Us", "Our"), made by Laxmi Solutions, handles your information when you use the Glossa app for Android (package `solutions.laxmi.glossa`). In short: **your learning progress stays on your phone**, or in your own Google Play Games save if you use it. Glossa has no account system, no servers that receive your progress, and no analytics. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (section 2.7); **Glossa Pro has no ads**.

---

## 1. What is Glossa?

Glossa is a word-search game for learning English vocabulary. You find words on a letter board, see what each word means, and review words over time with on-device spaced repetition. Optional features include a daily puzzle, streak reminders, Google Play Games achievements, leaderboards and cloud save, and Glossa Pro.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

**We do not collect personal information.** Glossa does not ask you to sign up, and it contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The free version includes Google's advertising SDK (section 2.7). The sections below explain what stays on your device and the connections Glossa makes on your behalf.

### 2.1 Your Progress and Settings (On Your Device)

The words you have learned, their review schedule, your streak, daily goal, reminder time, levels you have unlocked and other settings are stored **in Glossa's private storage on your phone**. We never receive them. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes them, unless they are kept in an Android backup (section 2.9) or in your Play Games save (section 2.4).

### 2.2 Answers in the Level Check

The short level check during setup runs **on your device**. Your answers are used only to choose your starting level and are not sent anywhere.

### 2.3 Level Downloads

Glossa ships with its first levels built in. Further levels are part of the app and are downloaded by **Google Play** (Play Asset Delivery) when you get close to them. Google Play handles these downloads under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); we have no server of our own, and **your progress is never sent**.

The free version is supported by ads, so starting a new level needs an internet connection after the first few levels. To apply this, Glossa checks on your device whether it is online. Nothing about this check is sent anywhere.

### 2.4 Google Play Games (Optional)

If you use Google Play Games Services, Glossa uses it for **achievements, leaderboards and cloud save**:

- **Achievements and leaderboards:** your achievements and scores (such as words mastered and longest streak) are sent to Google and may be visible to other players under your Play Games profile name, according to your Play Games settings.
- **Cloud save:** Glossa stores a copy of your progress (learned words, their review state, streak, unlocked levels and settings) in **Saved Games in your Google account**, so you can restore it on another device. We cannot access it.

Play Games is provided by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can manage or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or at [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com).

### 2.5 Purchases

Glossa Pro is a one-time purchase through **Google Play Billing**. Google handles payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether Glossa Pro is owned by your Google account and remembers the answer, and when it was last confirmed, on your device so Pro works offline. It checks again whenever the app is online, and at least once every seven days.

### 2.6 Pronunciation

The speaker button reads a word aloud using **your device's text-to-speech engine**. Only the word itself is passed to the engine. Which engine is used, and how it processes text, depends on your device settings.

### 2.7 Advertising (Free Version Only)

The free version of Glossa shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**. **Glossa Pro shows no ads.**

- **Full-screen ads between levels:** never during a puzzle, never in your first ten levels, never in reviews or the daily puzzle, and at most once every three levels.
- **Banner ads** at the bottom of the Journal and Progress screens.
- **App-open ads** when you come back to Glossa after a break: only after your first ten levels, after at least 30 minutes away, at most once every four hours, and never over a puzzle in progress.
- **Rewarded videos** that you can choose to watch for an extra hint, to unlock a single level of a Pro word pack, or to repair a streak you broke in the last two days.

To show, measure and limit ads, Google AdMob collects information from your device, such as your **advertising ID**, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This information goes **directly to Google**; we do not receive it, and your progress is never shared with advertisers. Google's use of this data is described in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Glossa asks for your **consent** before any ad is requested, using Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Ad privacy options**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's **Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

### 2.8 Streak Reminders (Optional)

Reminders are **off by default**. If you turn them on, Glossa schedules a daily check on your device and shows at most one notification, only when your streak is at risk. No reminder data leaves your device.

### 2.9 Android Backup

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up your progress and settings to your Google account, under Google's terms. Downloaded levels and the stored Pro status are excluded from backups.

### 2.10 Sharing Results

When you tap **Share**, Glossa opens Android's share sheet with a short text summary (for example the puzzle name and your streak). Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with.

## 3. Permissions

- **Internet and network state:** to download levels through Google Play, use Play Games and Google Play purchases, show ads in the free version, and tell whether you are offline (section 2.3).
- **Notifications:** only if you turn on streak reminders (section 2.8).
- **Advertising ID** (`AD_ID`): declared by Google's advertising SDK so ads can be measured and limited in the free version (section 2.7).

---

## 4. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect it. The only services Glossa communicates with are: Google Play (level downloads and purchases), Google Play Games (if you use it) and, in the free version, Google AdMob (section 2.7), which collects the advertising data described there directly from your device.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Everything Glossa stores is on your device or in your own Google account. Clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove everything on the device. Your Play Games save, achievements and leaderboard entries stay in your Google account until you delete them through Google Play Games.

---

## 6. Security

Network connections use HTTPS. Level downloads are delivered and verified by Google Play. Your progress is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we design Glossa to keep your data on your device.

---

## 7. Children

Glossa is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

---

## 8. Your Rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. For data held by Google (Play, Play Games, backups, AdMob), please use Google's controls; you can change your ad consent in **Settings → Ad privacy options** and manage your advertising ID in your device settings. If you have any question, contact us below.
