# Privacy Policy for Army Rush: Squad Surge

_Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Effective: November 17, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how Laxmi Solutions ("We", "Us", "Our") handles information when you play **Army Rush: Squad Surge** (the "Game"). By playing the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

---

## 1. Summary

- **No accounts.** The Game has no sign-up or login and never asks for your name, email, or phone number.
- **Your progress stays on your device.** Levels, coins, gems, skins, upgrades and settings are saved only in the Game's private storage on your phone.
- **The Game is free because of ads.** Ads are served through **Unity LevelPlay** and its ad partners. On first launch we ask whether ads may be **personalised**. You can change your choice any time in **Settings**. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.
- **Purchases go through Google Play.** We never see or store your payment details.
- **No analytics of our own.** We do not run our own analytics, and we have no servers that store information about you.
- **We do not sell your personal data.**

---

## 2. Information Stored On Your Device

The following is created and kept **only** on your device, in the Game's private app storage. It is never sent to us:

- **Game progress**: current level, highest level, coins, gems, keys, upgrades, unlocked and equipped skins, weapon tier, daily-reward streak, missions, spin-wheel and piggy-bank state.
- **Purchase records**: which in-game products you own, plus Google Play order identifiers, used only so that a purchase is never granted twice.
- **Ad counters**: how many rewarded ads you watched today, used to apply daily limits.
- **Settings**: sound on or off, and your **personalised-ads choice**.

This data is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its storage. Because it stays on the device, **progress does not move to a new phone** (purchases of permanent items can be restored, see Section 5).

---

## 3. Advertising

The Game shows **rewarded videos** (only when you choose to watch one, in exchange for an in-game reward), **interstitial ads** (full-screen ads between some levels), and a **banner** on menu screens. Ads are not shown during battles, and **Remove Ads** turns off interstitials and banners.

Ads are served by **Unity LevelPlay** (a Unity Technologies service) and the ad networks it works with ("ad partners"). To request, show, measure and protect ads against fraud, the LevelPlay SDK and ad partners may collect:

- the **advertising ID** of your device (Android Advertising ID);
- **IP address** and approximate location derived from it (country or city level);
- **device information**: model, operating system, language, screen size, network type;
- **ad interaction data**: which ads were shown, clicked or completed.

**Your choice:**

- **Personalised ads ON**: ad partners may use this data to show ads based on your interests.
- **Personalised ads OFF**: we tell LevelPlay you have **not given consent** (GDPR) and that you **opt out of the sale or sharing** of personal information (CCPA/CPRA). Ad partners then serve contextual, non-personalised ads, but may still process limited data to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud.

You can also **reset or delete your advertising ID** at any time in Android **Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads** (wording varies by device). Ad partners process data under their own privacy policies: see [Unity's Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

---

## 4. Internet Connections

The Game connects to the internet to:

- **load ads** (Section 3);
- **check that you are online**, by sending a small request to a Google server (`clients3.google.com`). The reply also gives the current time, used for daily rewards and events;
- **download level and event updates**, as small data files from our content server hosted on Amazon Web Services. These requests contain no personal information, but, like any web request, they reveal your IP address to the hosting provider;
- **process purchases** with Google Play (Section 5).

---

## 5. Purchases

In-app purchases (for example Remove Ads, skin packs, coin and gem packs) are handled entirely by **Google Play Billing**. The Game checks the purchase receipt on your device and records the order identifier locally. **We never receive your payment details.** Permanent items can be restored on a new installation with **Shop ▸ Restore** while signed in to the same Google account.

---

## 6. Children

The Game is intended for a **general audience aged 13 and over** and is **not directed at children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

## 7. Data We Do NOT Collect

- No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise (GPS) location.
- No accounts, chat, or user-generated content.
- No analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.

## 11. Your Rights

- **Change your ad choice** any time in **Settings ▸ Personalised ads**.
- **Reset or delete your advertising ID** in Android settings.
- **Delete all Game data** by clearing the Game's storage or uninstalling it.

### European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland (GDPR)
Personalised ads rely on your **consent**, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings. You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data on our own servers, requests about ad data are best sent to Unity using the link in Section 3; we will help if you contact us.

### California and other US states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws)
We do not sell personal information for money. Personalised advertising may count as "sharing" under these laws; turning **Personalised ads** off opts you out.

### India (DPDP Act, 2023)
You may withdraw consent and request erasure as described above, and contact us with any grievance.
