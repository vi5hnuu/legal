# Privacy Policy for MathPro

_Last updated: July 18, 2026 · Effective: July 18, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how MathPro ("We", "Us", "Our") handles your information when you use our application. By using MathPro, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. What is MathPro?

MathPro is an Android calculator with several modes — standard, scientific, programmer, percent, worksheet, unit converter, graphing, finance, and date tools. MathPro runs **entirely on your device**. There is no MathPro account and no MathPro server: your calculations, history, and settings never leave your phone except through Google's advertising services described below.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Data
**None.** MathPro has no sign-up and no login. We do not collect your name, email, or any account identifier.

### 2.2 Calculation History
When you press equals, MathPro saves the expression, its result, and a timestamp to a **capped local history** (the most recent entries only). This history is stored **only in your device's private app storage**, is used solely to let you search, reuse, and pin past calculations, and is never transmitted to us. You can delete individual entries or clear the whole history from within the app.

### 2.3 App Settings
Your preferences — theme (light/dark/system), accent colour, angle unit, and last-used mode — are stored **only in your device's private app storage** so the app opens the way you left it. These are never transmitted to us.

### 2.4 Advertising Data
MathPro is free and shows ads served by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address, and usage signals to serve and measure ads, in accordance with [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, MathPro shows a Google-certified consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are served.

### 2.5 What We Do Not Collect
MathPro does **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera input, or the contents of other apps. It requests **no** sensitive or dangerous Android permissions — only network access, which is used solely to load ads.

## 4. Data We Do NOT Collect

MathPro is explicitly designed so that:

- **Your calculations and history never leave your device.** They are stored only in the app's private storage.
- **Your settings stay on your device.**
- **We have no account and no server**, so we hold no profile of you.

## 5. Sharing Your Data

We share data only in the following circumstances:

| Recipient | What is shared | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Google LLC** | AdMob advertising identifiers/signals | Advertising |
| **Law enforcement / courts** | Only data we actually hold (which is minimal) | Legal obligation |
| **Acquirer (if the app is sold)** | Any applicable data | Business transfer — users will be notified |

We never share your calculations, history, or settings, because we never collect them off-device.

## 7. Your Rights

Because MathPro stores your data only on your device, you control it directly:
- **Access / delete** your calculation history in-app (per entry or clear all), or remove everything by clearing app storage or uninstalling.
- **Withdraw or change ad consent** at any time via the consent options where offered, and adjust ad personalisation in your device's Google settings; you can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

### India — Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP)
We process your data only for the purposes described above and support correction or erasure requests at the email below.

### European / UK / Swiss Users (GDPR / UK GDPR)
You additionally have rights to access, correct, erase, object to processing, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Ad consent in these regions is collected via Google's User Messaging Platform.

## 8. Data Security

- MathPro keeps your history and settings in your device's private app storage.
- No calculations, history, or settings are transmitted off-device — the strongest possible protection for that data.
- Communication with Google AdMob uses encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.
